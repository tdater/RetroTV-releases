RETROTV

Every retro console on your Android TV, and not one emulator to install. RetroTV is a TV browser built for the sites that run NES, SNES, Genesis, Game Boy, N64, PlayStation, arcade and DOS games right in the page.

Pick a site with the remote, plug in a controller and play. It handles the parts a phone browser gets wrong on a TV: a real pointer for the remote, controllers that reach the game, ads that don't hijack the screen, and full screen that actually fills the TV. ▮

Remote & controllers NO MOUSE NEEDED

Web emulators expect a mouse and a keyboard. RetroTV gives the remote a real pointer and gives controllers straight to the game, with up to four players.

The remote

D-padMoves the pointer; it speeds up while held. Push against an edge to scroll.
OKClick. Hold OK and move to drag.
Hold OK / MenuQuick menu with every control.
BackPrevious page; leaves full screen; exits.
CH+ CH−Scroll a page at a time.
Play/PauseSwitch to D-pad keys: arrows and Enter go to the game, so you can play with just the remote.

Controllers

  • Gamepad API in every frame. Your pad appears as a standard gamepad to the page and to emulators inside iframes, even on TVs whose WebView doesn't support gamepads.
  • Keyboard mode for games that only read the keyboard: each button sends a key you choose (arrows, Z/X/A/S, Enter, Shift, F-keys…).
  • Both at once, chosen per site and remembered.
  • Analog sticks, triggers and hat switches; up to 4 controllers.
  • Select + Start opens the menu. Pad moves pointer lets the controller browse: stick moves, A clicks, B goes back, shoulders scroll.

Windowed or full screen FILL THE TV

Windowed

A slim toolbar stays on top with back, reload, home, the address, blocked-ad count and input mode. The pointer can click it.

Full screen

The page gets the whole TV. Sites' own full-screen buttons work too, and Back leaves them.

Focus game

One button finds the game (canvas, emulator frame, Flash/Ruffle player) and blows it up to fill the screen at the right aspect ratio, hiding everything else.

Ad blocking that leaves games alone PER SITE

Free game sites are full of pop-unders and "click anywhere" redirects. RetroTV blocks those without touching what you came for.

What's blocked

  • Ad servers, pop-under networks and trackers, at the network level.
  • Pop-up tabs and automatic redirects to other sites.
  • Leftover empty ad boxes (optional).
  • Links that try to open other apps or the Play Store.

What never is

  • The page you open, and the site's own menus and links.
  • Game files and emulator code (archive.org, EmulatorJS, js-dos and the common CDNs are on an allow-list).
  • Anything you want: blocked pop-ups wait in the menu under Open blocked pop-up, and one switch turns blocking off for a site.
  • Add your own rules (hosts-file and ||host^ lines work).

Userscripts, one click away GREASY FORK BUILT IN

Find

  • Open Userscripts from the menu and the scripts made for the site you're on are already listed.
  • Search Greasy Fork by keyword, or tap a quick search: ad block, anti-adblock, fullscreen, gamepad, dark mode.
  • Tapping a .user.js link anywhere offers to install it.

Manage

  • Turn scripts on or off, remove them, or check them all for updates.
  • Scripts run in every frame at their @run-at time, with the common GM_* helpers.
  • One switch turns all scripts off.

Install SIDELOAD THE APK

RetroTV isn't on the Play Store. Install the APK from the latest release (under Assets).

Google TV / Android TV

  1. Install the free Downloader app (AFTVnews) from the Play Store on the TV.
  2. Open it and enter this page's address, or the release link, then download the RetroTV APK.
  3. When asked, allow Downloader to install unknown apps, then press Install.
  4. RetroTV appears in your apps row.

Fire TV

  1. Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options → turn on Install unknown apps for Downloader. (No Developer options? Settings → My Fire TV → About, then press OK on the device name 7 times.)
  2. Install Downloader from the Amazon Appstore and use it to download the APK.
  3. Press Install, then open RetroTV from Your Apps.

Needs Android 7.0 or newer. Games run on the TV's Android System WebView; if games stutter, update it from the Play Store. Installing emulator apps from inside RetroTV asks once for permission to install apps.

RetroTV is a browser. The games, emulators and ads belong to the websites it opens, which are independent third-party sites and not affiliated with RetroTV; what's available and legal to play depends on each site and on where you live. Emulator apps download from their own developers.
